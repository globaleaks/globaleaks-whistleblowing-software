Introduction
============
.. include:: badges.rst

GlobaLeaks is a free and open-source software platform that makes it easy for anyone to set up and operate a secure, anonymous whistleblowing/reporting site. Designed with privacy and user safety in mind, the platform is highly customizable and aims to lower the barriers for launching trustworthy whistleblowing initiatives. You can learn more at the official website: `GlobaLeaks <https://www.globaleaks.org/>`_.

Key Features:
-------------
- **Open Source & Community-driven:** Freely available with no licensing costs. All source code, documentation, and security specifications are public and regularly peer-reviewed.
- **Transparent & Secure:** Undergoes independent security audits and public code review to ensure user confidence.
- **Widely Adopted:** Used around the world in anti-corruption, corporate compliance, human rights, and investigative journalism.
- **Global Recognition:** Listed as a `Digital Public Good <https://digitalpublicgoods.net/r/globaleaks>`_ by the `Digital Public Good Alliance <https://digitalpublicgoods.net>`_.
- **Sustainable:** Maintained and supported by the social enterprise `Whistleblowing Solutions <https://www.whistleblowingsolutions.it>`_.

GlobaLeaks is based on well-defined :doc:`design principles <getting-started/design-principles>` and is designed to be open, adaptable, and secure. The project’s global impact is made possible by a vibrant community of contributors, users, and supporters.
